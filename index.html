FAQ
Good questions.

      

        
How do you confirm my bKash payment?
I check the Transaction ID against my bKash account by hand, then confirm with you on WhatsApp.

        
What if I'm not selected?
If your application isn't selected, or the episode isn't published, I refund ৳8,000 to the bKash number you paid from. ৳2,000 is kept as a processing fee.

        
Will I see the questions before the shoot?
I share the main topics before we record. The exact questions stay natural, so the conversation feels real. Topics you listed as off-limits won't come up.

        
Does paying guarantee a positive or scripted episode?
No. Paying covers the feature and the production. The conversation stays honest, and the episode is labelled as a paid feature on YouTube.

        
Who owns the episode, and can I upload it?
The Podcast owns the recording. You can share it anywhere and upload it to your own pages with credit, as long as you don't monetize it. Details are in the terms.

        
Studio or online, which is better?
Both work. The studio gives the best picture and sound. Online is easier if you're outside the city or abroad.
